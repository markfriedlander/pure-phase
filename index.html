Pure Phase

Breathe. Focus. Calm. Sleep.

Synchronized light, sound, and breath, tuned to guide you into focus, calm, or sleep. Tap to begin. Hold to tune. That is the whole interface.

Four states

Breathe for breathwork, Focus at 40 Hz, Calm at 10 Hz, Sleep at 2 Hz. Each pairs a gentle light flicker with a matching audio pulse.

Sound from nothing

Every tone is generated on your device in real time. A warm carrier pulses at the target frequency, synchronized frame by frame with the light. No audio files.

Guided breath

A breathing ring paces Coherence, 4-7-8, Box, and Slow Wave patterns, or build your own. No flicker, just the breath.

DRIFT and BLOOM

For headphones. DRIFT builds depth and slow movement from pure tones, no flicker at all. BLOOM adds a generative field of light that breathes and shifts color with the sound.

Torch for sleep

Sleep can pulse your iPhone's torch through closed eyelids, screen dimmed, for eyes-closed entrainment.

Yours, and free

Free. No subscription, no account, no narration, no streaks, no ads, and no data collection of any kind.
