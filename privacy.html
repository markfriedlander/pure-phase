PURE PHASE

privacy policy

Pure Phase does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data of any kind.

All session activity — including your chosen states, breath patterns, audio settings, and session duration preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage. Nothing leaves your device. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or health data. The flashlight (torch) feature accesses your device's camera hardware only to control the torch light — no image or video data is captured or processed.

Pure Phase is free, contains no advertising, and has no commercial relationship with any third party that would involve your data.
